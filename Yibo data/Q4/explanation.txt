Male hospitalisations remained substantially higher than female hospitalisations throughout 2011–2021. Male cases increased from 22,321 in 2011 to 25,788 in 2021, while female cases increased from 11,712 to 13,716. Males consistently accounted for approximately two-thirds of hospitalisations.The 2020 decline was much larger among females: female hospitalisations fell from 14,499 in 2019 to 12,781 in 2020, while the male count declined only slightly from 25,365 to 25,178. These are counts, not sex-specific population rates or exposure-adjusted risks.
