In 2021, people aged 40–64 recorded the largest number of hospitalised road traffic injuries (13,026), followed by those aged 26–39 (9,423) and 17–25 (8,010). Together, these three groups accounted for approximately 77.1% of all hospitalisations.The age bands have unequal widths; for example, 40–64 covers 25 years while 65–74 covers 10 years. These absolute counts therefore do not show an individual’s age-specific risk.
