First Nations hospitalisations increased across all remoteness categories, but at different rates. Between 2011 and 2021, Major Cities increased from 368 to 795 cases and Regional areas from 455 to 885, while Remote areas increased more slowly from 330 to 503. Consequently, the Remote share declined from 28.62% to 23.04%, whereas the Major Cities share increased from 31.92% to 36.42%. Regional areas remained the largest category, at approximately 40%.
