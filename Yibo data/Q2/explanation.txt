In 2021, Victoria recorded the highest number of hospitalised road traffic injuries (11,130), followed by Queensland (10,447) and New South Wales (9,003). Together, these three jurisdictions accounted for approximately 77.4% of the national count. The territories recorded substantially lower counts, with the Northern Territory reporting 710 and the Australian Capital Territory 740.These figures are absolute counts rather than population-standardised rates. Therefore, lower counts in smaller jurisdictions do not necessarily indicate lower individual risk.
