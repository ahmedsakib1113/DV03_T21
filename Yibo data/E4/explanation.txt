Road-user composition did not move in the same direction across Australian jurisdictions. Cyclist shares increased in New South Wales (+6.42 percentage points), Victoria (+5.32), South Australia (+5.86), Western Australia (+4.33), and especially the Australian Capital Territory (+17.25), but changed little or declined in Queensland, Tasmania, and the Northern Territory. Vehicle-occupant shares declined in several jurisdictions, including New South Wales (−7.87), but increased in Queensland (+7.54), Tasmania (+5.54), and the Northern Territory (+5.59). Pedestrian shares declined across every jurisdiction, while motorcyclist changes were mixed. The comparison windows differ because of data comparability: New South Wales begins in 2017, Victoria in 2012, and other jurisdictions in 2011, with all ending in 2021. Suppressed n.p. cells were retained as missing rather than zero; therefore, some aggregated vehicle-occupant shares in smaller jurisdictions may be slight underestimates.
