During 2016–2021, the groups contributing the most hospitalisations did not always contribute the most bed days. People aged 40–64 accounted for the largest shares of both hospitalisations (32.82%) and bed days (34.17%). However, people aged 75 and over represented only 6.23% of hospitalisations but 13.29% of bed days. Similarly, those aged 65–74 accounted for 7.31% of hospitalisations but 11.18% of bed days. This suggests that hospitalised injuries among older adults may require longer stays or greater healthcare resources. Conversely, people aged 8–16 accounted for 6.61% of hospitalisations but only 3.53% of bed days.
