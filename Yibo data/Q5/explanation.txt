Vehicle occupants recorded the largest number of hospitalisations in 2021 (19,524), representing approximately 49.4% of the national total. Motorcyclists accounted for 8,883 cases (22.5%) and cyclists for 8,163 (20.7%). Pedestrians recorded 2,334 cases (5.9%). These counts do not account for differences in travel frequency, distance travelled, or population exposure. They therefore describe the composition of hospitalisations, not the risk per trip or kilometre.
