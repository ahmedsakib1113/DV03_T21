The First Nations pattern differed from the national shift toward pedal cyclists. The selected cyclist share declined slightly, while the motorcyclist share rose substantially. Geographically, growth was concentrated more strongly in Major Cities and Regional areas than in Remote areas. The road-user analysis uses four consistently published categories; suppressed n.p. categories were not treated as zero. Its percentages therefore represent shares among the four selected groups, not all First Nations injuries. The raw tables contain First Nations and Non-Indigenous categories but no status-not-stated category, so unknown-status records cannot be reassigned and totals should not automatically be equated with national totals.
