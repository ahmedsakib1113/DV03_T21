Hospitalized Road Traffic Injuries in Australia

BITRE data · 2011–2021

Key questions Elective questions

Five key research questions

National trend States and territories Age groups Sex comparison Road-user groups

National trend

How did hospitalized road traffic injuries in Australia change between 2011 and 2021?

States and territories

How do hospitalized road traffic injuries vary across Australian states and territories?

Age groups

Which age groups account for the highest numbers of hospitalized road traffic injuries?

Sex comparison

How do hospitalized road traffic injuries differ between males and females?

Road-user groups

How do injury numbers differ among road user groups, such as vehicle occupants, motorcyclists, cyclists, and pedestrians?

Elective questions

National road-user share Age by road user Hospitalizations and bed days Jurisdiction trends First Nations patterns

National road-user share

To what extent did the 2011–2021 change in national hospitalised road injuries reflect a shift in road-user composition, rather than a uniform rise and fall across road users? Did the 2020 disruption leave pedal cyclists at a higher share in 2021?

Age by road user

In which age-by-road-user groups did hospitalised injury counts rise between 2011 and 2021? Did the increase among older adults occur among pedestrians, or among car drivers and pedal cyclists?

Hospitalizations and bed days

Within the comparable window 2016–2021, do the road users and age groups that contribute the most hospitalisations also contribute the most bed days?

Jurisdiction trends

Inside each jurisdiction’s comparable period — Victoria from 2012, New South Wales from 2017, and the other jurisdictions across 2011–2021 — did road-user composition move in the same direction?

First Nations patterns

Did the rise in First Nations hospitalised injury counts follow the national shift toward pedal cyclists, or a different road-user and remoteness pattern? Which comparisons remain valid once status-not-stated and suppressed cells are taken into account?
