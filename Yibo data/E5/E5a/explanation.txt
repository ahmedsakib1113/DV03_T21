First Nations road-user composition did not follow the national shift toward pedal cyclists. Among the four consistently published road-user groups, the cyclist share decreased from 10.95% in 2011 to 9.76% in 2021. In contrast, the motorcyclist share increased substantially from 16.92% to 24.53%. Car occupants remained dominant at approximately 55%, while the pedestrian share declined from 16.29% to 11.08%.
